Withholding: actual wording, actual procedural state

The law excludes embarrassment, reputational harm and political sensitivity as grounds. It permits specified segregable protections, including victim information and narrowly tailored temporary active-case withholding, and requires written redaction justifications published in the Federal Register and submitted to Congress. Agency assertions and lawful application are separate questions. Public Law 119-38, §§2–3.
